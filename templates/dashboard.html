What do these numbers actually mean?
Time open
How long the document was open in their browser. Someone can leave a tab open and walk away, so a large number here on its own does not prove much.
Actively working
How long they spent moving the text cursor — typing, clicking, selecting. Every burst of movement earns about half a minute, so the figure is built from moments of activity rather than measured continuously. Scrolling and reading without touching anything move no cursor and earn nothing, which means a careful reader can show very little here. Read it as a lower bound on involvement, never as a total.
Edits
Changes Google attributed to them in the document's own revision history. We do not calculate these, we only read them back, which is what makes them persuasive if the split is ever questioned.
When the three disagree
That is informative, not a fault. Lots of time open with no activity and no edits means a tab was left running. Edits with no time open usually means our watcher was offline while they worked, and the edits are still valid.
Is anything missing?
Edits are recorded retroactively and go back to the day the document was created. Presence is only recorded while the watcher is running, so any gap in the banner above is a gap in the presence numbers, never in the edit numbers.
